# Northwind Home Returns and Refunds Policy

This policy covers returns of Northwind Home products and the payment of refunds. All amounts are in US dollars.

## 1. Return Window

Most products bought directly from Northwind Home (through our online store, the customer portal or a Northwind retail store) can be returned within 30 calendar days of delivery for a full refund, for any reason.

**Holiday period.** Products delivered between November 15 and December 24 can be returned until January 15 of the following year.

Products purchased from an authorized retailer must be returned to that retailer and are subject to the retailer's own return policy.

## 2. Condition of Returned Items

Unopened items are refunded in full, including the original standard shipping charges.

Opened items in working condition are accepted within the 30-day window. A restocking fee of 15% applies to opened items with a purchase price above $200, unless the item is defective or was delivered damaged. Opened items priced at $200 or below have no restocking fee.

Returned items must include the original accessories, manuals and packaging where reasonably possible. Missing accessories are deducted from the refund at the accessory's current list price.

## 3. Items That Cannot Be Returned

The following items cannot be returned unless they are defective:

- Gift cards and digital subscriptions that have already been activated, including CarePlus plans after 14 days from purchase.
- Devices professionally installed by a Northwind installation partner, once the installation is marked complete.
- Clearance items marked "Final Sale."
- Items damaged by the customer after delivery.

## 4. Damaged or Incorrect Deliveries

If a product arrives damaged or you receive the wrong product, report it through the customer portal within 7 days of delivery and include photos of the packaging and the product. We will send a replacement at no cost and provide a prepaid return label.

Reports made after 7 days are handled under the Limited Warranty Policy (NWH-POL-001) instead.

## 5. How to Start a Return

1. Sign in to the customer portal and open Orders.
2. Select the order and choose Start a Return.
3. Pick the item, the reason for the return and the refund method.
4. Print the prepaid return label, or choose drop-off at a Northwind retail store.
5. Ship the item within 14 days of starting the return. Return requests expire after 14 days.

Return shipping is free for defective, damaged or incorrect items. For change-of-mind returns, a flat return shipping fee of $7.99 is deducted from the refund.

## 6. Refund Timelines

Refunds are processed within 5 business days of the item being received and inspected at the returns center. After processing, the time it takes for the refund to reach you depends on how you paid:

| Payment method | When the refund is available |
|----------------------|----------------------------------------------------------|
| Card | Typically appears on your statement within 3 to 7 business days, depending on your bank |
| Northwind store credit | Immediately after processing |
| PayPal | Usually visible within 2 business days |

If you paid with a combination of a gift card and a card, the refund goes to the gift card first, up to the amount originally paid with it.

## 7. Exchanges

We do not process direct exchanges. To exchange a product, return the original item and place a new order. If you want to guarantee stock of the new item, you can place the new order before the return is processed.

## 8. Price Adjustments

If Northwind Home lowers the price of a product within 14 days of delivery, you can request a one-time price adjustment for the difference through the customer portal. Price adjustments do not apply to flash sales lasting less than 24 hours, retailer prices or bundle discounts.

## 9. Refund Disputes and Escalation

If your refund has not arrived within 12 business days of the return being marked "Received," contact support. You can start in the portal chat, and the virtual assistant will transfer you to a refunds specialist who can trace the payment and, where needed, issue store credit while the investigation continues.
