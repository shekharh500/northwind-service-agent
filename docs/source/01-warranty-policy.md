# Northwind Home Limited Warranty Policy

This policy sets out the terms of the Northwind Home Limited Warranty. In this policy, "Northwind Home," "we" and "us" mean Northwind Home, and "you" means the owner of the device.

## 1. Scope of This Warranty

1.1 This Limited Warranty covers all Northwind Home branded devices purchased new from Northwind Home or an authorized retailer ("Covered Devices").

1.2 The covered product lines are the Aura Smart Thermostat range, the Halo Video Doorbell range, the Beacon Smart Speaker range and the Pulse Smart Plug range.

1.3 Accessories such as mounting plates, power adapters and cables are covered for a shorter period, as described in Section 3.

1.4 Refurbished devices sold through the Northwind Certified Refurbished program carry a separate 12-month warranty. They are not covered by the standard terms of this policy unless this policy explicitly states otherwise.

## 2. Standard Warranty Period

2.1 All Covered Devices carry a standard warranty of 24 months from the date of original purchase, as shown on the proof of purchase.

2.2 If you register your device in the Northwind Home app or on the customer portal within 30 days of purchase, the warranty is automatically extended to 36 months at no extra cost. Registration after 30 days does not qualify for the extension.

2.3 The warranty period is not reset or extended when a device is repaired or replaced. A replacement device inherits the remaining warranty period of the original device, or 90 days, whichever is longer.

## 3. Accessories and Consumables

3.1 Power adapters, mounting plates, wall plates and cables are covered for 12 months from the date of purchase.

3.2 Batteries in the Halo Video Doorbell are covered for 12 months or 500 charge cycles, whichever comes first. A battery that retains less than 70% of its original capacity within that period is considered defective.

3.3 Consumables, such as adhesive mounting strips, screws and decorative faceplates, are not covered.

## 4. What the Warranty Covers

The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship under normal residential use. Examples of covered faults include a device that will not power on, a display that shows dead pixels or lines, a Wi-Fi radio that repeatedly fails to connect to a supported network, a temperature sensor that reads more than 2 °C away from a reference thermometer, and buttons or touch panels that stop responding.

## 5. What the Warranty Does Not Cover

The warranty does not cover:

(a) damage caused by accidents, drops, liquid spills or submersion, except where the product is rated for outdoor use and the damage occurred within its stated IP rating;

(b) damage caused by incorrect installation, including wiring a thermostat to line-voltage (120V or 240V) heating systems that are not listed as compatible;

(c) cosmetic damage, such as scratches, dents or discoloration, that does not affect function;

(d) devices used in commercial, industrial or rental-fleet settings;

(e) devices with a removed, altered or unreadable serial number;

(f) faults caused by third-party software, unofficial firmware or modifications;

(g) loss of service caused by internet outages, router faults or third-party platform changes; or

(h) normal wear and tear, including gradual battery aging beyond the limits in Section 3.

## 6. Remedies

6.1 If a covered defect is confirmed, Northwind Home will, at its discretion, repair the device, replace it with a new or equivalent refurbished device, or refund the purchase price. A refund under the warranty is offered only when neither repair nor replacement is possible.

6.2 The maximum amount payable under any single warranty claim is the original purchase price of the device, excluding shipping, installation charges and taxes that are not refundable by law.

## 7. How to Make a Warranty Claim

7.1 To make a claim, follow these steps:

1. Open a support case through the Northwind Home customer portal or the in-app chat assistant.
2. Provide the device serial number, the date of purchase and a proof of purchase (receipt, invoice or order number).
3. Complete the guided troubleshooting steps. Many issues are resolved by a firmware update or factory reset.
4. If the fault persists, a support specialist will issue a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number within 2 business days.
5. Ship the device using the prepaid label provided. Devices sent without an RMA number will be returned unprocessed.

7.2 Warranty claims are normally completed within 10 business days of the device arriving at the Northwind service center.

7.3 If you have an active CarePlus Premium plan, you will receive an advance replacement, shipped before the faulty device is returned.

## 8. Claims Without Proof of Purchase

If proof of purchase is not available, the warranty period is calculated from the manufacturing date encoded in the serial number, plus 90 days.

## 9. Transfer of Warranty

9.1 The warranty may be transferred once to a new owner if the device is sold privately.

9.2 The new owner must re-register the device and upload the original proof of purchase.

9.3 The 36-month registration extension transfers only if the original owner registered the device within 30 days.

## 10. Contact

Warranty questions can be raised through the customer portal assistant at any time. Live support specialists are available Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 8 p.m., and Saturday, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. (local time). During these hours, a conversation with the virtual assistant can be escalated to a live specialist.
