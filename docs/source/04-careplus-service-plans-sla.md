# Northwind Home CarePlus Service Plans and Support Service Levels

## 1. Plan Overview

CarePlus is an optional protection and support plan for Northwind Home devices. It is available in two tiers, CarePlus Basic and CarePlus Premium, and can be purchased at the time of device purchase or within 60 days afterward.

CarePlus Basic costs $4.99 a month or $49 a year and covers up to 5 registered devices. CarePlus Premium costs $9.99 a month or $99 a year and covers up to 15 registered devices.

## 2. Plan Comparison

Prices are in US dollars and apply per household.

| | CarePlus Basic | CarePlus Premium |
|--------------------|--------------------------------|--------------------------------|
| Price | $4.99 per month, or $49 per year | $9.99 per month, or $99 per year |
| Registered devices | Up to 5 | Up to 15 |
| Accidental damage protection | Not included | Up to 2 claims per 12-month period, with a service fee of $29 per claim |
| Replacement type | Standard replacement after the faulty device is received | Advance replacement shipped before the faulty device is returned |
| Support channels | Chat assistant 24/7; live chat and email with specialists during business hours | Chat assistant 24/7; priority live chat; phone callback; one free professional installation per year |

## 3. Support Service Levels

Northwind Home aims to meet the following first-response targets for cases raised by customers.

| Plan | Live chat (during business hours) | Phone callback | Email |
|-------------------------|------------------------|----------------------|----------------------|
| Customers without a plan | Within 10 minutes | Not available | Within 2 business days |
| CarePlus Basic | Within 5 minutes | Not available | Within 1 business day |
| CarePlus Premium | Priority live chat within 2 minutes | Within 1 hour | Within 4 business hours |

Business hours are Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 8 p.m., and Saturday, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m., local time.

The virtual assistant is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Outside business hours, live specialists respond only to Priority 1 safety issues (see Section 4). Any other conversation that needs a specialist outside business hours is converted into a support case, and the customer is contacted at the start of the next business day.

## 4. Case Priority Levels

| Priority | Applies to | Target |
|-----------------|----------------------------------------|----------------------------|
| Priority 1 (Critical) | Safety issues such as overheating, a burning smell, or smoke from a device. Customers must disconnect power immediately. | A specialist responds within 30 minutes, 24/7, for all customers regardless of plan |
| Priority 2 (High) | Device completely non-functional; heating or cooling unavailable | Resolution within 2 business days |
| Priority 3 (Medium) | Partial loss of functionality, connectivity problems, app issues | Resolution within 5 business days |
| Priority 4 (Low) | How-to questions, feature requests, cosmetic issues | Resolution within 10 business days |

## 5. Transfer to a Live Specialist

A chat with the virtual assistant is transferred to a live support specialist when:

- the customer asks to speak to a person;
- the issue is a Priority 1 safety issue;
- the customer asks for a refund exception, a goodwill credit or another policy exception;
- the assistant has not been able to answer the customer's question after two attempts; or
- the customer says they are frustrated or unhappy with the service.

The virtual assistant passes a short summary of the conversation to the specialist when it transfers the chat.

## 6. Canceling a Plan

Monthly plans can be canceled at any time from the customer portal and remain active until the end of the current billing month.

Annual plans canceled within 14 days of purchase receive a full refund. Annual plans canceled after 14 days receive a prorated refund for the remaining full months, minus any accidental damage claim service fees that were waived.

## 7. Exclusions

CarePlus does not cover loss or theft, intentional damage, devices used commercially, or cosmetic damage that does not affect function. Accidental damage claims beyond the annual limit are charged at the standard out-of-warranty repair price.
